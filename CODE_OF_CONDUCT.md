# Code of Conduct

LVS connects people who help each other in the real world. We expect the same respect here.

- Be respectful and constructive. No harassment, hate speech, threats or personal attacks.
- Do not post other people's private data: addresses, phone numbers, photos or documents.
- No spam, scams, fake giveaways or impersonation of the LVS team.
- Keep issues on topic: bugs, questions and suggestions about LVS.

Content that breaks these rules may be removed, and repeated violations may lead to a block.

Report problems to [support@lvs.network](mailto:support@lvs.network).
