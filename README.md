# LVS — Living Value System

**People get paid for real things they do in the real world, and every result is proven.**

A photo of a shop sign, a check on site, a short live video from a place — someone nearby
does it, the app proves where and when it was done, and the person who asked pays.

[app.lvs.network](https://app.lvs.network) · Telegram: [@LVSNetworkBot](https://t.me/LVSNetworkBot)

---

## What LVS does

- **Tasks near you.** Find tasks around you or post your own, with a deadline and a price.
- **Proof, not promises.** Photos, videos and live calls are captured inside the app and
  bound to the place and the time. Faces and number plates can be hidden on request.
- **Fair outcomes.** Accepted work is paid at once. If the requester stays silent, the worker
  is paid automatically. Disagreements are decided from the data — place, time, media —
  not from words.
- **VU** — the in-app unit for paid tasks: 1 VU = 1 USDT/USDC. Deposits and withdrawals go
  to your own crypto wallet.
- **BON** — bonus units for trying LVS: tasks for BON, never money, never transferable.
- **Private by design.** Chats and media between the two sides of a task are end-to-end
  encrypted. Servers relay and coordinate; they do not keep your media.
- **30 languages**, with paid live translation of chat and video between the two sides.

## How it is built (in short)

LVS is a peer-to-peer network with a shared core that runs on every device. Each action is a
signed event; every device checks the same rules, so no single server decides alone.
Money movements are recorded in a ledger with independent verification, and keys that can
move funds are never kept on the application servers.

## Status
